Who We Are

We are a volunteer foundation established to protect the endangered wild tiger population and support their natural habitats.

How We Protect

We are on the ground every single day, keeping a close eye on these beautiful big cats. From setting up safe shelters to using smart tech that watches over them while they sleep, we do whatever it takes to make sure they can roam free and safe.
